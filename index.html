Machine Learning

Early Prediction of High-Potential Banking Customers

A bank does not need to wait a full year to know which new customers are worth investing in. For my MSc thesis, I trained Random Forest and Logistic Regression models on 4,500 real bank accounts to test whether 90 days of transaction data can predict a customer's future value as reliably as waiting twelve months, reaching a ROC-AUC of 0.86 and validated with bootstrap significance testing.
